## **<u>Contacting us for general questions</u>**

Want to get the fastest response time? [<u>Our discord server</u>](https://discord.gg/invite/rjj6W6QYVG) is THE place to be! Apart from getting in touch with \+100K fellow CubeCrafters you can also reach out to our whole CubeCraft team in the #team-help channel, using the designated language buttons you can also get in touch with team members who naively speak your language.

You can email us at `support@bloodfallsmp.qzz.io` 

## **<u>Contacting a Specific Team Member</u>**

Contacting a specific team member is perfect in situations where you want more clarity on a previous interaction with them, e.g. for a closed player/bug report or a rejected appeal.

Join [Our discord server](https://discord.gg/invite/rjj6W6QYVG) to contact a staff member by creating a ticket
