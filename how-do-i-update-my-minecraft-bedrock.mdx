---
title: "How do I update my Minecraft Bedrock?"
---

This article will help you learn how to update your Minecraft Bedrock to the latest version. In order to update your Bedrock Minecraft game you must have a legal copy of Minecraft: Bedrock Edition.

## How do I update?

- As Minecraft Bedrock is on many devices, updating the game can vary. You will need to go to the place on your device where you would normally download the Minecraft game. When there, if there is a game update available, click "Update". Once your game is updated, you can start Minecraft Bedrock and you'll be on the latest version!
