---
title: "How do I update to Java 1.21.x or above?"
---

This article will help you learn how to update your Minecraft Java version to 1.21.x or above. In order to update your Java Minecraft game you must have a legal copy of Minecraft: Java Edition.

## How do I update?

- Open your Minecraft Client, this is also known as "Minecraft Launcher".

<Frame>
  ![Chrome 2019 07 30 14 15 15 1c1mo1n](https://storage.crisp.chat/users/helpdesk/website/7bb7fd711237c800/image_1ijmcv5.png)
</Frame>

- Click on the 'Installations' tab.

<Frame>
  ![Chrome 2019 07 30 14 15 15 1c1mo1n](https://storage.crisp.chat/users/helpdesk/website/7bb7fd711237c800/image_1yihlyc.png)
</Frame>

- Click on 'New' and add a name for your new version.

- Select the version you would like use, you can choose between 1.21.x and up

<Frame>
  ![Chrome 2019 07 30 14 15 15 1c1mo1n](https://storage.crisp.chat/users/helpdesk/website/7bb7fd711237c800/image_1nbz9yg.png)
</Frame>

- Click on 'Create' in the bottom-right corner to save your changes.

- Return to the play tab and select the version you want to play.

</Frame>
