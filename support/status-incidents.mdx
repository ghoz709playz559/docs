We believe in keeping our community informed whenever issues arise and providing updates as quickly as possible.

## Status Page

Our official status page provides real-time information about server services, website availability, and ongoing incidents.

Status Page:\
[https://status.bloodfallsmp.qzz.io](https://status.bloodfallsmp.qzz.io)

You can view current system health, scheduled maintenance, and previous incident reports.

## Discord Announcements

Major outages, maintenance windows, and important service updates are also announced in the official BloodFall SMP Discord server.

Joining our \[Discord\](https://discord.gg/) is the fastest way to receive community updates and communicate with staff during an incident.

## Server Components

The status page may report issues affecting specific services, including:

- Minecraft Server
- Website & Forums
- Authentication Services
- Discord Integrations
- Support Systems

Not all incidents affect every service. Reviewing the affected component can help determine how the issue impacts you.

## Scheduled Maintenance

Occasionally, maintenance is required to improve stability, performance, and security.

Whenever possible, maintenance windows will be announced in advance through both the status page and Discord server.

## Incident Updates

If an unexpected outage occurs, staff will provide updates as information becomes available.

Please avoid opening support tickets solely to report a known outage, as this can slow response times for other players requiring assistance.

## Incident Reports

For major incidents, we may publish a summary explaining:

- What happened
- Which services were affected
- Actions taken by staff
- Measures implemented to prevent similar issues

## Reporting Issues

If you experience a problem that is not listed on the status page, please submit a support ticket with as much detail as possible, including:

- Your Minecraft username
- Time the issue occurred
- Error messages (if any)
- Screenshots or evidence

This helps our team investigate and resolve issues more efficiently.

Thank you for your patience and support while we work to keep BloodFall SMP running smoothly.
