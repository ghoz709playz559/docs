Welcome to the **BloodFall SMP** help page. Everything you need to join, play, and get unstuck lives here.

## How to join

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check the Minecraft version">
    Make sure your Java Edition client matches the server version. Mismatched versions will fail to connect.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the server">
    In Minecraft, open **Multiplayer → Add Server** and paste the server address:

    ```text
    play.bloodfallsmp.example
    ```

    Replace this with your real server IP once it's live.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get whitelisted">
    BloodFall is a whitelisted SMP. Request access in our Discord with your exact Minecraft username.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Join and read /rules">
    Once whitelisted, connect and run `/rules` in chat before you start building.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Server rules

<Card title="The short version" icon="scroll">
  Be kind, don't grief, don't cheat. If you're unsure, ask staff first.
</Card>

1. **No griefing or stealing** from other players' builds or chests.
2. **No hacked clients, x-ray, or exploits.** Optifine and approved QoL mods are fine.
3. **Keep chat friendly.** No harassment, slurs, or doxxing.
4. **Respect claims.** Always ask before building near someone.
5. **PvP is opt-in** outside of designated arenas.
6. **Don't lag the server** with massive redstone or mob farms — check with staff before building one.

Breaking rules can result in warnings, temp bans, or a permanent ban depending on severity.

## Common commands

| Command | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `/spawn` | Teleport back to spawn. |
| `/sethome <name>` | Save a home location. |
| `/home <name>` | Teleport to a saved home. |
| `/tpa <player>` | Request to teleport to another player. |
| `/tpahere <player>` | Ask a player to teleport to you. |
| `/msg <player> <text>` | Send a private message. |
| `/claim` | Claim the chunk you're standing in. |
| `/rules` | View the full rules in chat. |
| `/help` | List available commands. |

## Getting help in-game

- Ask in public chat — most players are happy to help.
- Use `/helpop <message>` to ping online staff.
- For anything sensitive, DM a staff member on Discord instead of public chat.

## Reporting a player

If someone is breaking the rules:

1. **Don't retaliate.** Screenshot or record what happened.
2. Open a ticket in the Discord `#support` channel.
3. Include: your username, the offender's username, time (with timezone), and evidence.

Staff will review and respond as soon as possible.

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I can't connect to the server">
    - Confirm you're on the correct Minecraft version.
    - Double-check the server address for typos.
    - Make sure you're whitelisted.
    - Try restarting your client and router.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I'm getting kicked for lag or timeout">
    - Test your connection on speedtest.net.
    - Lower your render distance to 8–10 chunks.
    - Close bandwidth-heavy apps in the background.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I lost items to a bug or crash">
    Open a ticket in Discord with your username, what you lost, and roughly when it happened. Staff can roll back inventories in clear bug cases.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="My claim isn't working">
    Stand inside the chunk you want to claim and run `/claim`. Use `/claimlist` to see what you already own and `/abandonclaim` to release one.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
