Having trouble signing into your BloodFall account? This guide covers the most common login problems for both the BloodFall website and Android app.

If you're still unable to access your account after following these steps, please contact support.

## Account Portal

You can manage your BloodFall account through:

[https://passport.bloodfallsmp.qzz.io/](https://passport.bloodfallsmp.qzz.io/)

This portal allows you to sign in, manage your account, and update account information.

## Incorrect Email or Password

Double-check that:

- Your email address is entered correctly.
- Your password is typed correctly.
- Caps Lock is not enabled.
- You are using the account originally linked to BloodFall.

If you cannot remember your password, use the password reset option.

## Password Reset

If you've forgotten your password:

1. Visit the BloodFall Passport Portal.
2. Select **Forgot Password**.
3. Enter your account email.
4. Follow the instructions sent to your inbox.

If multiple reset emails are requested, use the most recent link provided.

## Verification Emails Not Arriving

If you do not receive account verification or password reset emails:

- Check your Spam or Junk folder.
- Verify that you entered the correct email address.
- Wait a few minutes for delivery.
- Check any email filtering rules you may have configured.

If emails still do not arrive, contact support.

## Android App Login Problems

If the BloodFall Android App is not allowing you to sign in:

1. Close the application completely.
2. Reopen the app.
3. Verify that you have a stable internet connection.
4. Update to the latest version of the app.
5. Attempt to sign in again.

If the issue persists, try logging in through the Passport Portal to verify that your account is accessible.

## Session Problems

If you are repeatedly signed out or redirected during login:

### Website

- Clear your browser cookies and cache.
- Disable browser extensions that may interfere with authentication.
- Try using a private/incognito window.
- Test another browser.

### Android App

- Clear the app cache.
- Restart your device.
- Reinstall the application if necessary.

## Account Locked or Restricted

Accounts may be temporarily restricted for security reasons or policy violations.

If you believe your account has been restricted incorrectly, submit a support ticket for review.

## Still Need Help?

When contacting support, please include:

- Your Minecraft username
- The email address associated with the account
- Screenshots of any error messages
- The device and browser/app version being used

Providing this information helps us diagnose login issues more quickly.
