Experiencing issues on BloodFall SMP? This guide covers the most common problems players encounter and the steps you can take before contacting support.

If your issue is not listed below, feel free to submit a support ticket.

## Cannot Join the Server

### Check the Server Status

Before troubleshooting, verify that the server is online by checking:

- The BloodFall SMP Status Page
- Official Discord announcements

The server may be undergoing maintenance or experiencing an outage.

### Verify the Server Address

Make sure you are connecting using the correct server IP:

`mc.bloodfallsmp.qzz.io`

Typos or outdated addresses can prevent you from connecting.

### Check Your Minecraft Version

Using an unsupported Minecraft version may result in connection errors.

Verify that you are using a version currently supported by BloodFall SMP.

## High Ping or Lag

### Check Your Internet Connection

Restart your router or switch to a more stable connection if possible.

### Close Background Applications

Programs that use large amounts of bandwidth may affect your gameplay experience.

Examples include:

- Downloads
- Streaming services
- VPNs
- Other online games

### Server Performance

If multiple players are reporting lag, the issue may be server-related. Check the status page or Discord for updates.

## Authentication Errors

If you receive authentication-related messages:

1. Log out of Minecraft.
2. Close the launcher completely.
3. Log back into your account.
4. Restart Minecraft and reconnect.

Temporary Mojang or Microsoft service issues can occasionally cause authentication failures.

## Missing Items or Progress

If you believe items, balances, claims, or progress have disappeared:

- Relog to the server.
- Restart your Minecraft client.
- Check if a recent rollback was announced.

If the issue persists, create a support ticket with as much information as possible.

## Discord Linking Issues

If Discord integration is not working:

- Verify that your Discord account is connected correctly.
- Ensure you completed any required verification steps.
- Rejoin the Discord server if necessary.

If problems continue, contact support and provide screenshots of any error messages.

## Appeal or Punishment Questions

Questions regarding bans, mutes, warnings, or other punishments should be submitted through the Appeals section rather than General Support.

Staff will review each appeal individually.

## Reporting Bugs

When reporting a bug, include:

- Your Minecraft username
- What happened
- What you expected to happen
- Steps to reproduce the issue
- Screenshots or videos (if available)

Detailed reports help us resolve issues faster.

## Still Need Help?

If none of the solutions above resolve your issue, submit a support ticket and a member of the BloodFall SMP Staff Team will assist you as soon as possible.
