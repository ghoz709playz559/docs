## Support Categories

### General Support

For gameplay questions, account issues, server features, or any assistance that doesn't fit another category.

### Player Reports

Report players who may be breaking server rules. Include evidence whenever possible, such as screenshots, videos, or chat logs.

### Punishment Appeals

If you believe a ban, mute, or other punishment was issued unfairly, you may submit an appeal for staff review.

### Bug Reports

Found a bug or exploit? Let us know so we can investigate and fix the issue as quickly as possible.

## Before Submitting

Please include:

- Your Minecraft username
- A clear description of the issue
- Screenshots or video evidence (if applicable)
- Steps to reproduce the problem
- Relevant dates and times

The more information you provide, the faster we can assist you.

## Evidence & Attachments

When reporting players or server issues, attach any available evidence. Reports without sufficient evidence may take longer to investigate.

## Response Times

Our staff team reviews requests as soon as possible. Response times may vary depending on ticket volume and staff availability.

Please avoid creating multiple tickets for the same issue, as this can delay support for everyone.

## Follow-Up Information

If you discover new information after submitting a ticket, reply to your existing ticket instead of creating a new one. This helps keep all relevant details in one place and allows staff to assist you more efficiently.

## Respectful Communication

Please remain respectful when communicating with staff members. Harassment, spam, or abusive behavior may result in your ticket being closed and could lead to further action.

Thank you for helping us keep BloodFall SMP a friendly and enjoyable community for everyone.
