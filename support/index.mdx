This help desk contains everything you need to get assistance, understand server policies, and resolve common issues. Whether you're appealing a punishment, reporting a player, experiencing technical problems, or looking for answers to frequently asked questions, you'll find the appropriate resources here.

## What We Can Help With

- Player reports and rule violations
- Ban, mute, and punishment appeals
- Technical issues and bug reports
- Account and gameplay support
- Server policies and guidelines
- General questions about BloodFall SMP

## Before Opening a Ticket

Please check our FAQ and available documentation first, as many common questions already have answers. Providing detailed information, screenshots, or evidence when submitting a report will help our staff assist you more efficiently.

## Response Times

Our staff team reviews requests as quickly as possible. Response times may vary depending on request volume, complexity, and staff availability.

## Community First

BloodFall SMP is committed to providing a fair, enjoyable, and welcoming experience for all players. Thank you for helping us maintain a positive community.

If you need assistance, browse the categories in the sidebar or submit a support request through the appropriate section.
