---
title: "How do I report a player?"
---

Whilst our moderation team and in house anti-cheat try to catch all cheaters before they wreak too much havoc, some still slip through the cracks. That's why we recommend players to report these cheaters either in game or on our forums/reports website.

- In game: Much quicker and easier for players, but may not be handled in time if no moderator is online.
- Forum report: Requires (video) evidence, but that the report is handled.

<Note>
  Please refer to our [<u>reporting guidelines</u>](https://help.bloodfallsmp.qzz.io/support/troubleshooting/index) before submitting a report. Not following our guidelines will result in an **denied report**.
</Note>

<Warning>
  If you feel unsafe on BloodFall SMP, you can read more about our community guidelines, resources and player safety [<u>here</u>](https://www.cubecraft.net/forums/community-resources-safety.121)
</Warning>

## How do I report players in game?

1. First, join our Minecraft server
2. Then run:
   ```text
   /report <player> <reason>
   ```
3. After that, a staff will review your report shortly.

## How do i report a player in discord?

1. Join our discord server by pressing Discord server in our home page
2. Goto [◟💌・support・](https://discord.com/channels/1492205493232341192/1492395199496720424) channel
3. Then click the Report Player button

Now you've successfully made a player report! Well done and thanks a lot for helping us! We really appreciate it
