### **<u>What is a blocked connection?</u>**

Blocked connections have been developed in order to reduce the amount of rule breaking, more specifically cheaters who evade their bans by switching IP addresses.

<Card title="You can find your error code when you log into the server. If you see a different screen, then your connection is likely not blocked." type="info">
</Card>

### **Why is my connection blocked?**

Your IP address is being blocked by Sentinel, receiving a blocked connection does not mean that account was caught cheating on BloodFall SMP, but rather that a different account using the same IP connection (such as WiFi or VPN connection) has an active punishment. The duration of the IP block is automatically calculated by Sentinel, based on the amount of other accounts that are associated with the current and previous IP addresses, along with the punishments they have received. The duration of these IP blocks can vary between minutes and months.

<Warning>
  **Please do not ask team members about the duration of your IP block—this is information we can not provide to you.**
</Warning>

### **How can my connection be unblocked?**

You will have to wait for your IP block to expire, a block is non-appealable. You might have been IP blocked because you are trying to access our SMP on an alt account to bypass a previous punishment, or because someone on your network has been punished by our team. Once the IP block expires, you will be able to join the server. Please do not ask team members to reduce or remove these blocks, as they are handled by an automated system and will not be modified by our team members.

- If you were recently unbanned and are still seeing a blocked connection screen, please allow up to one day for the block to automatically expire.

**Team Members cannot:**

- Tell you how long the block will last.
- Remove the blocked connection from your account.
- Offer any alternative options to bypass your connection block.
